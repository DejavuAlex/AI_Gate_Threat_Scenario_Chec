# Localized Laboratory Platform Business Flow

1. A customer administrator enters the 内网管理门户 through the management network.
2. The administrator selects an 离线安装包导入 operation.
3. The platform verifies package provenance before staging the update.
4. A user performs 企业目录认证 through the customer directory.
5. Privileged changes, package validation, and authentication decisions are audited locally.
